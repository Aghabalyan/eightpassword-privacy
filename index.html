Privacy Policy

8Pass is a password manager for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac. Your vault is encrypted on your device and we can’t read it. This policy explains exactly what does leave your device, and why.

02 You decide what leaves it iCloud sync and Apple Wallet passes only happen when you use them.
03 No ads or tracking No advertising, no analytics tools, no tracking across apps or websites.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how 8Pass (formerly 8Password; “8Pass,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the 8Pass apps for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, the 8Pass AutoFill extensions, and the 8Pass Shortcut web page. By using 8Pass, you acknowledge the practices described here.

2. Your 8Pass account

To use 8Pass you create an account with your email address and a master password. Accounts are managed with Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google.

  • Email address. Used to sign you in and identify your account. Our account system may also send you an automated email when you create your account and when you delete it.
  • Master password. On your device, 8Pass turns your master password into a separate sign-in secret, and only that secret is sent to Firebase Authentication. Accounts created with earlier versions of the app may still sign in with the master password itself; it is sent over an encrypted connection, and Firebase stores only a one-way hash of it, never the password.
  • Account record. We keep a small record for your account in Firebase Realtime Database. It contains your email address; whether you have an active subscription and when it expires; a random identifier for your app installation (accounts created with earlier versions may instead hold the identifier Apple gives to apps from the same developer); and, for purchases made in earlier versions of the app, the App Store transaction identifier.

We use this information to sign you in on your devices and to restore your subscription. Your account record never contains anything from your vault.

3. Your vault

Your vault is everything you save in 8Pass: logins, verification codes, cards, codes, NFC tags, notes and other items. It is stored in a database on your device, which the 8Pass AutoFill extension on the same device can also read.

  • Item names, field names and field values are encrypted on your device before they are saved, using a key that is created on your device and kept in the device Keychain.
  • A few organizing details are stored without that encryption: each item’s category, whether it is a favorite or shown on Apple Watch, and when it was created and last edited.
  • Your vault is not sent to us, and we cannot read it. If you forget your master password, we cannot recover it or your data.
  • Backups of your device (to iCloud or a computer) may include the encrypted vault.

4. iCloud sync (optional)

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on, and it requires 8Pass Premium. When it is on:

  • Each item is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it is uploaded. The key is created on your device and shared with your other devices only through iCloud Keychain, which Apple protects with end-to-end encryption.
  • The encrypted items are stored by Apple in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. Apple and we can see only what is needed to sync: record identifiers, timestamps, revision numbers, deletion markers, and a one-way fingerprint of the key. Neither Apple nor we can read the contents of your items.
  • 8Pass can show you a recovery code that starts with “8PSK.” It is your sync key in readable form: anyone who has it and access to your iCloud data could decrypt your synced vault, so keep it somewhere safe.

You can turn sync off at any time in the app’s settings. iCloud is provided by Apple under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. Apple Wallet passes

On iPhone you can add a card or code from your vault to Apple Wallet. Apple requires every pass to be signed by the app’s developer, so 8Pass sends the contents of that one pass to our pass-signing service when you tap Add to Apple Wallet. It also does this when you edit a card whose pass is already in Wallet and the pass needs to change. The request contains:

  • what appears on the pass: the card’s name, the business or organization, the member name and number, the barcode or QR code content, the expiration date, the colors, and any template fields you filled in (for example an event’s date, venue and seat);
  • any artwork or logo image you chose for the pass, as a redrawn copy without the original photo’s location or other metadata;
  • a random serial number for the pass, a sign-in token that identifies your 8Pass account, and an App Check token that confirms the request comes from the genuine app.

Passwords, notes and your other items are never sent. The service runs on Google Cloud Functions (Firebase) in the European Union. It builds the signed pass in memory, returns it to your device, and does not store the pass or its contents. Its logs record only technical details about each request, such as whether it succeeded, the pass style, the barcode type and the first characters of the serial number, never the card’s contents. Google also keeps standard request logs, such as IP address and device type, for a limited period.

A barcode or QR code is sent exactly as it is stored, so don’t add a code to Wallet if it contains a secret, such as a Wi-Fi password. Once a pass is in Apple Wallet, Apple handles it under its own privacy policy.

6. Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app (a Premium feature), your iPhone sends your favorites and the items you mark for Apple Watch, including their passwords and verification-code secrets, directly to your watch. They are encrypted with a key created on the watch and are stored on the watch only in encrypted form. They do not pass through our servers.

7. AutoFill

The 8Pass AutoFill extensions read your vault on your device to fill in passwords and verification codes when you choose an entry. So your device can suggest the right entry, 8Pass adds each login’s website and username, and for verification codes the item’s name, to the system’s AutoFill list on your device. Passwords and code secrets are never added to that list. AutoFill makes no network connections.

8. Device features and permissions

  • Face ID, Touch ID or Optic ID. Handled entirely by Apple. 8Pass only learns whether authentication succeeded and never receives biometric data.
  • Camera. Used to scan QR codes and barcodes. Only the decoded text is used; no photos or video are taken or stored.
  • Photos. When you pick a picture with the system photo picker, to read a QR code from it or to use as pass artwork, 8Pass receives only the pictures you pick. A picture read for a QR code is discarded afterwards; pass artwork is kept as a redrawn copy inside the encrypted item, and synced if iCloud sync is on.
  • NFC. When you scan a tag, its technical details and contents are saved as an encrypted item in your vault. When you write a tag, 8Pass writes the content you choose. Anyone with an NFC reader can read what is written on a tag.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, your device registers with Apple’s push notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging, which issue a device token. We do not store this token and do not send marketing notifications. iCloud sync uses silent notifications to know when to update.

8Pass does not use your microphone, contacts or location.

9. 8Pass Shortcut links

An 8Pass Shortcut is an NFC tag that opens an item in 8Pass. The tag holds only a link of the form password-dc67e.web.app/s/… ending in a random code. The code is not derived from the item and contains no vault data.

On a device with 8Pass installed, the link opens the app directly. If it is opened on a device without 8Pass, a short web page is loaded from Firebase Hosting (Google), which receives standard request information such as the IP address, browser type and the link itself. The page has no scripts, analytics or cookies.

10. Subscriptions and payments

8Pass offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds and payment processing are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple, and the earlier version of the iPhone interface may send your App Store receipt to Apple to validate it. Your subscription status and expiration date are saved in your account record (section 2) so that Premium is restored when you sign in.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. Your use of 8Pass is also subject to Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement (EULA).

11. Advertising, analytics and tracking

8Pass shows no advertising, includes no analytics or crash-reporting tools, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

The Firebase services described in this policy may process technical information they need to work, such as your IP address, device and system type, and an installation identifier. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated crash and usage information, which we use only to improve 8Pass.

12. Service providers and where data is processed

We use these providers to run 8Pass:

  • Apple: iCloud, the App Store, Apple Push Notification service, App Attest and DeviceCheck, and Apple Wallet.
  • Google (Firebase): Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Cloud Functions and Hosting.

Your account record is stored in the United States. The pass-signing service runs in the European Union. Firebase and Apple may process data in other countries where they operate, under their own safeguards. We do not sell your personal information or share it with anyone for advertising.

13. Exports and files you create

You can export your vault and your encryption key from the app. A CSV export contains your passwords and other data in plain, unencrypted text, and a key export can be used to decrypt your vault. Where you save or send these files is your choice, so keep them safe and delete them when you no longer need them.

14. Retention and deletion

  • Your vault stays on your devices until you delete items, use Remove All Data, or delete the app.
  • Your iCloud copy stays in your iCloud until you use Remove All Data in the iPhone or iPad app, or delete 8Pass’s data in your iCloud storage settings.
  • Your account and account record are kept while you have an account. To delete them, use Settings → Remove All Data in the iPhone or iPad app. This deletes your vault from that device, your 8Pass data in iCloud, and your account and account record. Signing out, or deleting data in the Mac app, does not delete your account. If you can’t use the app, or the deletion could not finish (for example, because your device was offline), email us from your account’s address and we will delete it.
  • Pass-signing requests are not stored. Their technical logs are kept for a limited period under Google’s standard retention.
  • Support messages are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal obligations.

15. Security

8Pass encrypts your vault on your device, keeps its keys in the system Keychain, uses encrypted connections for every network request, and can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID, a PIN and auto-lock. On iPhone and iPad, copied passwords stay on that device and are cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds; on Mac they are cleared after the time you choose. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your devices, Apple Account and master password protected.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Your vault never reaches us, so you can view, edit, export and delete it yourself in the app. For your account record or support messages, contact us at the address below.

We use your account information to provide the service you asked for, and limited technical information to keep 8Pass secure and working. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

17. Children’s privacy

8Pass is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age required in their country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
